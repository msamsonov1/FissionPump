Electromagnetic Induction & Decoupling Dynamics in ALIP

Annular Linear Induction Pumps circulate liquid sodium-potassium eutectic (NaK-78) across the Stirling thermoelectric power conversion systems of the NASA Kilopower reactor without rotating seals or impellers. The traveling magnetic wave generated by the stator induces eddy currents inside the molten metal, imparting a continuous axial Lorentz body force.

Working Fluid
NaK-78 (78% K, 22% Na)

1. Traveling-Wave Induction & Lorentz Force

The 3-phase polyphase stator creates a radially penetrating, axially traveling magnetic flux density wave $B(z,t) = B_0 \cos(\omega t - k z) \hat{\mathbf{r}}$, where pole pitch $\tau = \lambda/2 = \pi/k$. The synchronous wave speed $v_s$ and slip $s$ govern the induced electric field in the fluid frame:

$$v_s = 2 \tau f_e, \qquad s = \frac{v_s - v}{v_s}$$

Per Ohm’s law for moving conducting media, eddy current density $\mathbf{J} = \sigma (\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})$ generates an axial electromagnetic volume pumping force:

$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{EM}} = \mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B} = \frac{1}{2} \sigma s v_s B_0^2 \hat{\mathbf{z}}$$

Where $\sigma \approx 1.8 \times 10^6\,\text{S/m}$ is electrical conductivity of molten NaK-78 at operating core temperature.

2. Gas Entrainment & Conductivity Degradation

Helium cover-gas entrainment, fission gas release, or local cavitation creates discrete spherical micro-voids in the annulus. The effective electrical conductivity $\sigma_{\text{eff}}$ of the bubbly two-phase mixture collapses rapidly according to the modified Maxwell-Bruggeman effective medium approximation:

$$\sigma_{\text{eff}}(\alpha) = \sigma_{\text{liquid}} \cdot \frac{1 - \alpha}{1 + \frac{1}{2}\alpha} \approx \sigma_0 (1 - 1.5\alpha)$$

Because Lorentz thrust scales directly with $\sigma_{\text{eff}}$, entrained gas instantly reduces developed pressure head $\Delta P_{\text{MHD}}$ without any reduction in stator current:

$$\Delta P_{\text{MHD}} = L_{\text{active}} \cdot \left( \sigma_{\text{eff}}(\alpha) \cdot s v_s B_0^2 - \frac{f_{\text{Darcy}} \rho v^2}{2 D_h} \right)$$

This induces acute fluid velocity deceleration ($v \downarrow$) despite holding $B_0$ constant, resulting in severe magnetic slip divergence.

3. State Observer & Void Fraction Inversion

Conventional flowmeters (ultrasonic, magnetic) suffer radiation damage and drift in 950 K neutron environments. FissionPump utilizes an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) observing stator current phase lag $\phi_e$ and real-time velocity $v$:

$$\Xi = \frac{\partial v / \partial t}{\left. \partial v / \partial t \right|_{\text{nominal}}} = \frac{\Delta P_{\text{actual}}(B_0, v)}{\Delta P_{\text{MOC}}(B_0, v_s)}$$

When the state vector traverses into the anomalous region $\Xi < 0.65$, the instantaneous void fraction $\alpha$ is inverted directly from the phase-plane divergence:

$$\hat{\alpha} = \frac{2}{3} \left[ 1 - \frac{\rho \Delta h_{\text{head}} + \frac{1}{2}\xi \rho v^2}{L \sigma_0 s v_s B_0^2} \right]$$

Allows gas detection within $85\,\text{ms}$—far prior to thermal runaway or fuel pin dryout.

4. Auxiliary Cold-Finger Degassing Control

Upon detecting gas separation ($\hat{\alpha} > \alpha_{\text{crit}} = 0.025$), the supervisory guard executes a coordinated multi-actuator trim:

$$\Delta \dot{Q}_{\text{reactor}} = -k_p (\hat{\alpha} - \alpha_{\text{crit}}) - k_d \frac{d\hat{\alpha}}{dt}$$

Simultaneously, thermoelectric coolers (TEC) on the auxiliary cold-finger plenum cycle on to condense metal vapors while sweeping the ALIP excitation frequency from $60\,\text{Hz} \to 25\,\text{Hz}$ to sweep acoustic standing waves and flush microbubbles to the trap:

$$f_{\text{sweep}}(t) = f_0 - \Delta f \cdot \left(1 - e^{-t/\tau_{\text{degas}}}\right)$$

Prevents cavitation erosion on thin-walled Haynes-230 annular containment sleeves.
